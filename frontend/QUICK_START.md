# Emergency Contact Management - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Getting Started (30 seconds)

### Step 1: Files Are Already Created ✅
Everything is ready to go! No additional setup needed.

### Step 2: Start Your App
```bash
npm start
```

### Step 3: Log In
1. Open the app in your browser
2. Click "Login / Register"
3. Create an account or log in

### Step 4: Access Emergency Contacts
1. Look for "Emergency Contacts" in the navigation menu
2. Click it to open the page

### Step 5: Add Your First Contact
1. Click "+ Add Emergency Contact"
2. Fill in the form:
   - Name: Your contact's name
   - Relationship: Choose from dropdown
   - Phone: 10+ digit phone number
   - Email: (optional)
3. Click "Add Contact"

**Done!** Your contact is now saved.

## 📋 What You Can Do

### Add Contacts
- Maximum 5 contacts per user

- Shows name, relationship, phone, email

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: In your browser's local storage. It stays on your device, not on a server.

**Q: Can other users see my contacts?**
A: No, each user has their own private list.

**Q: What if I clear my browser cache?**
A: Your contacts will be deleted. Avoid clearing cache if you want to keep them.

**Q: Can I export my contacts?**
A: Not yet, but this feature can be added in the future.

**Q: What if I log out?**
A: Your contacts stay saved. When you log back in with the same account, your contacts will be there.

**Q: Can I add more than 5 contacts?**
A: No, the limit is 5. Delete one to add another.

**Q: Do my contacts get notified?**
A: No, they don't get automatic notifications. You must contact them manually.

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Problem: Can't see "Emergency Contacts" link
**Solution:** Make sure you're logged in. The link only appears for logged-in users.

### Problem: Can't add more contacts
**Solution:** You've reached the limit of 5. Delete one to add another.

### Problem: Contact information not saving
**Solution:** Make sure to fill in all required fields and click "Add Contact".

### Problem: Contacts disappeared
**Solution:** 
- Your browser cache may have been cleared
- Try logging in again
- Check if you're on the same device and browser

### Problem: Phone/Email links not working
**Solution:** Make sure your phone/email app is set up on your device.
